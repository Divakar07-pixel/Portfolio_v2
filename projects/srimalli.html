Delivery Tracking.

A practical order-tracking experience for following deliveries from creation through final delivery.

THE PROBLEM

Customers need clear status, not scattered updates.

The workflow connects order creation, supplier dispatch, hub arrival, delivery assignment, out-for-delivery and delivered states into one understandable journey.

STATUS FLOWORDER JOURNEY
01CreatedOrder is registered
02DispatchedSupplier sends order

OPERATIONS

Designed around a simple lookup.

Customers can use an Order ID and mobile number to find the current status. The workflow also supports bill capture and operational delivery updates.
